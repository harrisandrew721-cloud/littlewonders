How we collect and handle personal and sensitive information.

We are required by law to give you this notice before, or at the time we collect your Personal Information and/or Sensitive Information. If it is not reasonably practicable for us to give you this notice at that time, we will give it to you as soon as we are able.

  3. Our Privacy Policy includes information about how you can access and correct your information, how you can complain about any breach of the Australian Privacy Principles and how we will deal with that complaint. The Privacy Policy is available on our website, Facebook page or upon request from Little Wonders Management.

If we transfer information outside Australia, we will comply with the requirements of the Act relating to transborder data flows. We will not directly disclose your information to overseas recipients without your consent, although entities to which we may disclose information may do so.
